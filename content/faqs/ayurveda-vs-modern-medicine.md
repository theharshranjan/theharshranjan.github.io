question: "What is Ayurveda and how is it different from regular medicine?"
answer: "Ayurveda is a 5,000-year-old Indian system of medicine that treats the root cause of disease rather than symptoms. Unlike modern medicine's one-size-fits-all approach, Ayurveda recognises that every individual has a unique constitution (Prakriti) — a combination of Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas — and tailors treatment, diet, and lifestyle accordingly."
