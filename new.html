    Welcome to Samarpan

    More Than Wellness.
    A Way of Living.

    At Samarpan, we believe wellbeing extends beyond the absence of illness. It is a continuous journey of understanding the body, nurturing the mind and creating harmony in everyday life.

    Bringing together Ayurveda, modern science, nutrition, yoga and mindful practices, Samarpan offers an integrated approach to healthier living.

    Frequently Asked

    Ayurveda is a traditional Indian system of health that is thousands of years old. It takes a whole-person view of wellbeing and recognises that every individual has a unique constitution (Prakriti) — a combination of Vata, Pitta and Kapha doshas — and tailors diet, lifestyle and practices accordingly. At Samarpan it complements, and never replaces, modern medical care.

    Yoga sessions focus on Hatha Yoga asanas, breathwork (Pranayama) and body awareness. Meditation sessions, including Ajapa Japa, work at a subtler level — training attention and cultivating inner stillness.
