This article was created entirely from the **admin panel** — no code was touched.

## How it works

1. A contributor logs into `/admin`
2. They fill in the title, description, image, tags, and body — right here in this box
3. They click **Publish**
4. This exact page, plus its card on the Journal listing, is generated automatically

## What you can do in the body

- Use **bold**, *italics*, and [links](https://thesamarpan.co.in)
- Add headings with `##`
- Make bullet lists like this one
- Everything renders using Samarpan's existing fonts and colors automatically

Once you're comfortable with how this looks, delete this sample article from the admin panel — it will remove this page and its card automatically on the next deploy.
