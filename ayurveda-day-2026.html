Why 23 September

"Balance between body, mind and nature."

Ayurveda Day was first marked in 2016 on Dhanwantari Jayanti, a date that shifts every year with the lunar calendar.

In 2025 the Government of India fixed it to 23 September every year. That is the autumnal equinox, when day and night are nearly equal, which suits a science built on balance.
